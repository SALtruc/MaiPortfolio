# Plan: Council / Club Day Authored Interactive Case (`CouncilCase`)

## Goal
Build a production-ready, bespoke interactive case study module for **RMIT Student Council — Club Day & community** (`src/cases/CouncilCase.tsx`, `src/cases/CouncilCase.css`, helpers under `src/cases/council/`, and design documentation at `docs/design-motion/council-experience.md`) that honors the project-experiences contract, stages an interactive 43-club campus constellation connected to CFO budgeting, sponsorship, and delivery supervision with real GSAP/ScrollTrigger path choreography, clearly separates event figures (`43` clubs and `2,500+` participants at Club Day S3/2025) from annual CFO stewardship (`500M+ VND` annual budget, `15+` monthly budgets/reimbursements) and wider council work (`20+` sponsors, `30+` stakeholders, `45` clubs across three semesters), and sequences real documentary photographs with full accessibility and reduced-motion support.

## Key Decisions
1. **Strict Scope Separation in Visual Hierarchy**: Rather than a flat 3-number banner that conflates event attendance with annual council finance, the achievement architecture explicitly separates (a) **Club Day S3/2025 ("Beyond the R-CADE")** (`43` clubs, `2,500+` participants, co-supervised with project leader and university staff), (b) **Annual Council CFO Scope** (`500M+ VND` annual budget, `15+` monthly budgets & reimbursements), and (c) **Wider 3-Semester Council Scope** (`20+` sponsors, `30+` stakeholders, `45` clubs in multi-semester reflection, and 2026 RMIT Impactful Leaders Award).
2. **Honest 43-Club SVG Constellation**: The signature interactive visual maps all `43` Club Day S3/2025 booths around `3` operational hubs (*Club Listening & Support*, *CFO Budget & Sponsor Commitments*, *Beyond the R-CADE Delivery Supervision*). Nodes are labeled `Club #01`–`Club #43` grouped into four real operational coordination tracks (never inventing club names or fake deliverables).
3. **Composable GSAP + Reduced-Motion Safety**: `useGSAP` and `ScrollTrigger` drive SVG connection path draw-ins (`strokeDashoffset`), hub pulse rings, and scroll-triggered section entrances when motion is enabled, while `paused` or `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` immediately presents all paths, nodes, and content in a clean static layout.
4. **Deliberate 5-Frame Documentary Photo Sequence**: Uses only authentic local assets (`/assets/council-meeting.webp`, `/assets/council-portrait.webp`, `/assets/clubday-portrait.webp`, `/assets/clubday-team.webp`, `/assets/student-council.webp`) arranged chronologically from committee meetings to CFO stewardship and Club Day S3/2025 delivery.
